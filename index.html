Asked at every demo

What is Pre-Approve Me?
A white-label app and marketing point of sale built for independent loan officers and small to mid-sized shops. Your brand goes on every screen, and the app stays with your buyer from the home search through closing and for years after.
Do my clients stay mine?
Yes. The app carries your branding, your buyers stay your clients, and we never market to them.
How does pricing work?
Standard is a progressive web app priced per seat with no setup fee. Native ships your own branded app to the Apple and Google app stores and carries a setup fee plus subscription.
Can buyers really issue their own pre-approval letters?
Yes. The qualification engine ties payments to the buyer's real financial picture, and buyers generate their own pre-approval letters from inside the app, on your letterhead.
What happens after the loan closes?
The app stays on their phone. Buyers keep watching their home's value and their equity in the place that carries your name, and the next transaction starts where they already are.

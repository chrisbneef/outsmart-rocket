Open the hood

Asked on every walkthrough

Why will my loan officers actually use it?
Because the three views map to moments they already live in, and because their buyers and agents pull them back into it. Adoption is a habit problem, not a feature problem, and the rollout is built around that: working app first, guided training second, depth only when they want it.
Who does the setup?
We do the configuration under your brand and switch on the core integrations live on the kickoff call. Your admins make decisions once at the company level, and the settings waterfall to every seat. Your producing LOs get founder-led training sessions, not a documentation link.
How does pricing work for a company?
Standard is priced per seat with no setup fee, so it scales with your roster. Native ships your company's own branded app to the Apple and Google app stores and carries a setup fee. We put exact numbers on the table during the walkthrough, mapped to your seat count.
Can branches and DBAs carry their own branding?
Yes. A parent company can run branded sub-companies for branches and DBAs, keep visibility across all of them, and still manage settings from the top.
What about our LOS and CRM?
Bring them. Some systems connect with full two-way sync, others work through a simple secure file import, and lead flows can be automated either way. We map your exact stack on the walkthrough before you commit.
